line; they moved by at most 2 ulp, which a different numpy or scipy in the rebuilt FEA venv can cause (`inferred`, not
traced). `results/or11/or11_tables.md` and the OR-12 table have no diff. The solver logs differ in dates and wall times
only. The table of the U-V7 record (b1 0.845525 T against embodic 0.846122 T at r 35.5 mm, -0.07 %; OR-12a
