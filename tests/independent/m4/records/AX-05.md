## Method

- Lever: `tests/independent/m4/ax_lever.py`. `render OUT` writes the D08 and wave SVGs with `write_svg`, renders each
  with QSvgRenderer at 12 px/mm on white, and cuts a 14 mm crop around each of the first 12 `coil-label` centres into a
  numbered contact sheet. `truth` prints, for each crop centre, the phase and polarity of the nearest top-layer copper
  path of the `Layout` (`embodic.api` outcome) and the next nearest other phase or sign.
- Greyscale: `PhaseRenderMode.GREYSCALE_HATCH` as written. Status `verified` (I looked at the PNG with the Read tool).
- Colour: `PhaseRenderMode.COLOUR`, then the Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes (2009) deuteranomaly matrix at severity
  1.0, applied to linear sRGB. Source: "A Physiologically-based Model for Simulation of Color Vision Deficiency", IEEE
  TVCG 15(6), 2009, pp. 1291-1298; Table 1 on https://www.inf.ufrgs.br/~oliveira/pubs_files/CVD_Simulation/CVD_Simulation.html
  (rows 0.367322 0.860646 -0.227968 / 0.280085 0.672501 0.047413 / -0.011820 0.042940 0.968881, read on that page
  this session). The page does not say linear or encoded RGB; linear is an `assumption`. The simulation is a proxy for a
  human reviewer with deuteranopia: status `inferred`.
- Order: I wrote down my readings from the sheets first and ran `truth` after.
- Reader: me, one reader, reading on a screen. Not a person with a colour-vision deficiency.

Rerun:


## Observations

- Polarity comes from the label text only, in both drawings and both topologies. Without the label a reader cannot
  tell A+ from A− (`verified` by looking: copper ink and eye fill do not change with sign). The labels are therefore
  load-bearing, which makes DEF-UV4-01 matter: the wave SVG names 44 of 48 belts (two B− and two C− belts carry no
  label, `verified` by counting the `coil-label` texts).
- Under the simulation, phase A (red) and phase C (green) both turn olive. They stay apart by lightness, dark versus
  light (`inferred`, by eye; design_spec.md l.110 gives deutan A-C 19.9 OKLab ΔE x100, the closest pair).
- In greyscale the interconnect ink (`phase-none`) is close to phase B's mid grey on D08. The labels and the B hatch
  separate them in every crop I read (`inferred`, by eye).
