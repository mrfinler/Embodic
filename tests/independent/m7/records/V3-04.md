My spellings of the plan's five mutants:

- V3-04-01 swaps the B_z and B_t terms of EQ-05 in `physics/field.py`. The product evaluates EQ-05 in its exponential
  form, so the cosh(u(G - z)) factor is `1 + e2b` and the sinh factor is `1 - e2b`. The mutant swaps the two. With an
  open rotor e2b is 0 and the swap changes nothing, so only a finite symmetry plane can kill it.
- V3-04-02 integrates `abs(c2 - c1)` instead of the signed dr in the EQ-06 flux linkage (`physics/emf.py`).
- V3-04-03 reads alpha_20 as 0.0039 instead of the registry's 0.00393 where `analyse()` computes the operating
  resistivity (`physics/analyse.py`). I seeded it there and not in `resources/evidence/equations.v1.json`, because the
  registry file carries a version hash that a test may check, which would kill the mutant for the wrong reason.
- V3-04-04 divides the EQ-12 eddy loss by 12 instead of 24 (`physics/emf.py` `eddy_loss_w`).
- V3-04-05 drops the last member of a series sum (`physics/circuit.py` `add_phasors`, the EQ-07 series sum).
