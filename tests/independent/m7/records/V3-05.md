## Verdict

3 of 4 mutants killed by their named tests. V3-05-01 survives the named test and the whole suite. Status `verified`
by running the lever.

The lever calls V3-05-03 `killed-other`: the named test fails with `IndexError: list index out of range`, not with an
assertion. The test splits the PDF text at "Key results" and takes the part after it
(`test_report_readme_bundle.py` l.64), so the IndexError means the heading is not in the PDF text, which is the
predicted disagreement. I count it as killed and leave the lever's word in the table. The plan excludes import errors,
and this is not one.

V3-05-01 survives, and on my reading it is an equivalent mutant for these inputs, not a test gap. The SVG writer
quantises to 1e-6 mm (`svg_writer.py` `_n`, six decimals). Joint points of the packaged examples' copper agree to about
4e-14 mm (the V3-03 probe printed worst joint gaps of 3.8e-14 and 4.0e-14 mm on the spiral examples). A start point
written from its own coordinate therefore gives the same six-decimal text as the shared pen position (unless a
coordinate lies within about 4e-14 mm of a rounding boundary), and the N-21
test, which compares the written numbers at every joint, cannot tell them apart. The extra `M` commands split a path
into subpaths, but the drawing group sets `stroke-linecap="round"` and `stroke-linejoin="round"` (`svg_writer.py`
l.339), so a split joint renders as a round cap over the same point. A joint whose two ends differ by more than the
quantum would expose the mutant. No such joint exists in the shipped examples (`inferred` from the V3-03 probe and the
six-decimal format, not measured on every SVG). No DEF row: the product's output for the shipped examples is the same
point either way.

The plan's criterion (100 % killed) is met for V3-05-02, -03 and -04 and not met for V3-05-01. I leave the ruling on
V3-05-01 to Fable.
