## Lever

`tests/independent/m7/v3_10_own_models.py [--json FILE] [--explain EXAMPLE ...]`. The product side is
`embodic.api.compute_project` on each file of `resources/examples/`, the path `embodic-cli compute` takes, and, for the
spiral examples, the winding plan the generator uses (`plan_spiral_winding`). The own side takes no expected value from
embodic:

- Star of slots and k_w,n from `m2/own_winding.py` (exact Fractions; EQ-08 text; k_p for a one-slot-pitch coil, the
  span the plan uses).
- Ke_LL from an EQ-05 field written in the script from equation_specification.md: cosh/sinh form, double-rotor
  G = (t_s + 2g)/2, EQ-04 alpha_p(r) = 2p asin(w/(2r))/pi for block magnets, span r_center +- length/2 for blocks. The
  EQ-06 sum and the chords (0.05 mm) come from `m2/own_emf.py`. Series groups add and parallel members average, as in
  `m3/gr22_own.py`. The geometry walked is the product's copper paths at the product's copper z, the M3 deviation.
- DRC from M1 and M3: each board goes through the product's dump writer, M1's reader and M1's certified pair sweep
  under the interfaces.md section 7 exemptions (`m3/drc_m3.universe`, `m1/drc_universe.sweep`). The rule of each pair
  kind is read from the design's `drc_rules` by the section 7 table (trace and pad-trace: trace clearance; any pair with
  a via, and pad-pad: via clearance; edge; hole).

The full output of the run is `V3-10_run.json` beside this record.

## How the Ke_LL bound settles

The plan's bound, Ke_LL within 1e-4 relative, was an `assumption`. The first run compared the product with the peak of
the continuous own line waveform (7201 points per electrical period) and failed on spiral_12c14p at -1.545e-4. I looked
for the limiter and tested three candidates (`--explain`, and an inline run of the same functions):

1. Own chord length. 0.05 mm to 0.02 mm moves own Ke_LL by at most 3.0e-7 relative on 12c14p, 12c16p and the
   serpentine example. Own chords at the product's `max_segment_mm` (0.25 or 0.5 mm) move it by at most 8.6e-6. Not
   the limiter.
2. The product's 48-step linear field table (`physics/emf.py` FIELD_TABLE_STEPS, Xcoil's NR). An own 48-step linear
   table moves the 12c16p gap from -1.20e-5 to -1.73e-6 and leaves 12c14p at -1.552e-4. It explains most of 12c16p, not
   12c14p.
3. The peak definition. EQ-18 names Xcoil `emfWaveform`, and `engine.js` l.1020-1028 samples one electrical period at
   181 points, 0 to 2 pi inclusive. The product does the same (`physics/waveform.py`, 181 samples). The largest sample
   of the own waveform on that grid gives the gaps in column 5: at most 2.46e-5 (D08). On 12c14p the 181-point sample
   lies 1.521e-4 below the continuous peak, which is the whole of its gap.

Settled. With the like-for-like quantity, the 181-point sampled peak the product reports, the own model agrees within
2.46e-5 on all five examples, so the 1e-4 bound holds with a margin of 4. The reported Ke_LL "peak" is a sampled peak.
It reads up to 1.52e-4 below the continuous waveform peak (12c14p). That is Xcoil's convention and EQ-18 adopts it.
Whether the label should say so, or the grid should be finer, is a ruling for Agent 2; I file no DEF row. The remaining
gaps (2.4e-6 to 2.46e-5) were not decomposed further. On 12c16p the 48-step table accounts for about 1.0e-5 of 1.2e-5
(`verified`). On the others the limiter is `unresolved`, below the bound.

## Verdict

- Phases: equal on the three spiral examples (`verified`). The serpentine and wave windings have no own star-of-slots
  model in M1 to M3 (their phases come from the layer and strand layout, not from a coil star), so for those two the
  star and k_w are `not tested`.
- k_w,n: within 8.9e-16 of the own value for n = 1, 3, 5, 7 on the spiral examples, inside 1e-12 (`verified`). The
  product's `winding_factor_1` in the outcome is EQ-27's utilisation factor k_u (quantity `utilisation_factor_k_u`), not
  EQ-08's k_w, so the plan's k_w comparison runs against the winding plan.
- Ke_LL: within 1e-4 on all five examples against the 181-point peak (`verified`). The continuous-peak comparison fails
  the bound on 12c14p for the sampling reason above.
- DRC: the product's pair violation sets equal the brute sweep's on all five boards, wave 186 (`verified`).

V3-10 passes on the rows it can model. Serpentine and wave phases and k_w are `not tested`.
