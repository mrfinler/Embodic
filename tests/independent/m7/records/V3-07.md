## Verdict

One counterexample in 10 seeds. The plan's criterion (0 failures over 10 seeds) is not met. Status `verified` by
running.

PR-05, seed 5 (`--hypothesis-seed=5`): `test_pr_05_arbitrary_values_never_raise_and_invalid_values_return_issues`
raised `ValueError: Out of range float values are not JSON compliant` in `compute` -> `design_hash` ->
`canonical_json`. The shrunk input is the CF-11 draft with a 12-sided polygon board (across flats 93 mm, corner radius
0) and `/outline/board/rotation_rad = 3.137566414384587e+306`. That value is finite. `math.degrees` of it is `inf`, so
the conversion to degrees overflows (`inferred` from the probe below and the `allow_nan=False` dump in
`io/canonical.py` l.71). Validation reports `RANGE_ABOVE_SUPPORTED` for 1e306 and 3.13e306 and compute returns. From the
found value up, compute raises. DEF-UV15-04.

    PYTHONPATH=src python tests/independent/m7/def_uv15_04_rotation.py
    found value x 180/pi = inf; math.degrees = inf
    rotation_rad 1e+306: no exception; issues ['RANGE_ABOVE_SUPPORTED']
    rotation_rad 3.13e+306: no exception; issues ['RANGE_ABOVE_SUPPORTED']
    rotation_rad 3.137566414384587e+306: ValueError: Out of range float values are not JSON compliant
    rotation_rad 1e+307: ValueError: Out of range float values are not JSON compliant

Other angle inputs in radians (bore rotation, layer-pair rotation, first coil angle, magnet offset) may share the
overflow. Not tested.

The scope caveats are part of the verdict. The nightly profile lengthens PR-08 to 2000 examples and PR-05 to 400. The
serpentine PR-13 and PR-14 stay at 3 (DEF-UV15-03). The other 33 tests do not depend on the seed. "20000 examples per
property" is therefore true for PR-08 only.
