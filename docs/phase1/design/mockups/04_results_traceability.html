Status labels

  • Status: verifiedChecked against evidence or reproduced; a computed result is verified only after it matched a validation oracle within tolerance.
  • Status: inferredComputed with an equation that passed the equation check; not yet validated against measurement.
  • Status: assumptionDepends on an input or constant chosen without evidence (e.g. copper thickness, film coefficient h).
  • Status: unresolvedEquation or input cannot be verified yet, or sources disagree; indicative only.
  • Status: not testedThe implementation or validation step has not been run.

A result shows the weakest status of its equation, inputs and validation (proposal; to be confirmed with Engineering and Verification).
